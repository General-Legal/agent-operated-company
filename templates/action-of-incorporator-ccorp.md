> **Drafting note — Markdown-only; do not copy into the DOCX or execution packet:** The first step in organizing the corporation: the incorporator adopts the Bylaws, names the initial director(s), and then resigns. For a single-founder company the incorporator is usually the founder (or counsel assisting with the formation). Pair this with the Organizational Resolutions. Complete every `{Field Name}` substitution and preserve the PandaDoc signature tags. Note: the company name must be used exactly as it appears in the Certificate of Incorporation, including the “, Inc.” suffix and punctuation.

**ACTION OF INCORPORATOR**

TAKEN WITHOUT A MEETING BY WRITTEN CONSENT

OF

**{Company Name Upper}**

{Effective Date}

Pursuant to Sections 107 and 108 of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware, the undersigned, being the incorporator (the “Incorporator”) of {Company Name}, a Delaware corporation (the “Corporation”), takes the following action by written consent:

1.  **Adoption of Bylaws.**

RESOLVED, that the Bylaws in the form attached as Exhibit A are adopted as the Bylaws of the Corporation.

2.  **Election of Director(s).**

RESOLVED, that {Director Name}, a natural person, is elected as the sole director of the Corporation, to serve until a successor is duly elected and qualified or until earlier resignation or removal.

Having adopted the Bylaws and elected the initial director(s), the undersigned Incorporator hereby resigns as incorporator of the Corporation, effective on the date set forth above. This Action of Incorporator shall be kept in the minute book of the Corporation.

{signature:incorporator_____}

{Incorporator Name}, Incorporator
